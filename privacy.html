Servicefolio Repair Finder — Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 30, 2026

Contact: christianbrain@gmail.com

Local information
The app keeps recent vehicles and saved video lists on your device. Lists may include the vehicle, model year, part, repair action, video IDs, titles, channel names, descriptions and thumbnail URLs. We do not operate an account or a server that receives your saved lists. You can delete individual saved lists in Saved. Removing the app may remove local information. We do not provide our own cloud-backup service; device backups depend on your operating-system settings. Video files are not downloaded.

YouTube and Google requests
Selecting a part sends your selected vehicle, year, part and repair action to Google through the YouTube Data API to obtain public video results. Refreshing saved results sends video IDs. Loading thumbnails contacts YouTube image servers. These requests expose your IP address and request information to those providers. Google handles information under its privacy policy. This app does not require Google sign-in or request access to your YouTube account. Opening a video takes you to YouTube's app or website, where its policies and your account settings apply.

Saved metadata is refreshed or cleared after 30 days when the app is used. Saved lists are metadata, not offline videos. Live search, thumbnail loading, refreshing and watching require a network connection.

No advertising SDK, analytics SDK, paid AI/TTS service or app-operated account backend is included in this build. This does not mean that no information reaches third parties: the requests described above reach Google/YouTube. We do not add advertising identifiers to those requests.

Questions and removal
Delete a saved list in the app to remove that local list. We cannot erase information held independently by Google/YouTube; contact that provider or use its privacy controls. For app questions, email christianbrain@gmail.com. Avoid sending confidential repair documents, passwords or full VINs in a support request.

Repair workspace: your checklists, user-entered costs, notes and outcomes are stored locally. No repair job is sent to Google or to our servers. Share job summary opens the system share sheet with the text summary you selected; the receiving app handles it under its own policy.

Vehicle discovery and search refinement: Global model labels may be unverified. Optional engine, generation/chassis code and vehicle market hints stay on the device until a video search sends them with the vehicle/part query to Google/YouTube. Do not enter a full VIN or personal details in these fields. Search-result text clues are not fitment confirmation.

You can delete individual repair jobs in Repair jobs. Uninstalling may remove jobs; shared copies must be deleted separately by their recipients.
